We live in the age of the centralized internet. It is bigger than ever in terms
of content, but looks and feels smaller and more uniform with every passing
year. Most of online discourse is concentrated within a handful of giant
platforms, which for many people comes with technical, ethical and
sociopolitical concerns. But it also makes the internet a ...bland and boring
place to be in.

"Great, but this is a BSD conference. What does BSD have to do with all this?"

The talk will be both philosophical, as well as technical. It will first go
through why people need to host their own platforms, and will then showcase how
BSDs are great at achieving that with minimal hassle, including a guide on how
to get started.
